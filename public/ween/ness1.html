NESS'S HALLOWEEN SPONTANZA!

By Radiation (2005)

original post from starmen.net 

CHAPTER I: NESS'S HALLOWEEN SPONTANZA!

Ness woke up. It was 5:00 PM, an hour before treak-or-treating time!
He was all like, "I gotta call Paula and Jeff and Poo and totally get
them to go trick-or-treating with me!" So he used telepathy to call
up Paula and Poo.
Paula said she'd be a cat and Poo said he'd be Mr. Saturn.
After, Ness called Jeff on his phone. One ring. Huh, Ness
thought, this can't be right, Jeff always picks up on the first ring.
Ring Two. Something is seriously wrong, he thought. Ring Three.
"COME ON JEFF, PICK UP THE PHONE ALREADY!!!" Then Jeff, dressed up as
an evil Jeff Clone came into the
room and picked up the phone. Then Ness remembered Jeff told Ness
yesterday he would come over at 5:00 PM so he wouldn't risk being late.
Ness was about to tell Jeff he was sorry, but he noticed Jeff was on the
phone, and it wouldn't be appropriate at the time.

CHAPTER II: CAN YOU DETECT A PATTERN!

Ten minutes later, Ness finished dressing up as a robot.
BRING! BRING! Paula and Poo must be here, he thought. Quickly, he hobbled
down the stairs and swung at the doorknob with his hand. He missed and
knocked the door down. To his horror it hit Poo and Paula, who were on the
other side. Fortunately for Ness, the door splintered on contact with Paula
and Poo, leaving them unfazed. "Uh... I thought you said you were gonna be
a cat and a Mr. Saturn for Halloween," Ness jabbered. "INCORRECT. YOU MUST
BE MISTAKEN. WE WERE ALWAYS GOING TO BE AN EVIL PAULA AND POO
CLONE."
Paula droned. "WHERE IS JEFF UNIT?" Poo continued. "Andonuts is upstairs
using the phone," Ness whispered. "It must be important because he's been
on there, for, like, an hour." "WELL THEN," Poo said flatly. "LET'S GO."

CHAPTER III: CANDY CANDY CANDY!

"Trick or Treat!" Ness shouted at Mr. Minch. "Here, take some Mounds and
leave," Mr. Minch said while gargling. "Sweet," Ness chuckled.
"MOUNDS BARS ARE INEFFECTIVE. WE NEED BATTERIES," Paula grated.
"AFFIRMATIVE," Poo jettisoned angrily. Paula swiftly pulled a giant laser
cannon out of her hair and blew up the house. "..." Ness yelled.
This process was repeated for the next 142 houses they stopped at.

CHAPTER IV: TICKET HOLDER

"LET'S GO UP TO OLD MAN GIYGAS' HOUSE," Paula and Poo lampshaded.
"Sure, as long as you two aren't too... scared," Ness guffawed.
"IT IS IMPERATIVE THAT WE ENTER THE HOUSE AND ACQUIRE THE..."
Paula stopped and looked for a word. Then she looked in her
candy bag and saw a word she could use. "CANDY. YES... CANDY."
"THAT IS THE TICKET," Poo coughed. So it was decided they would all
go up to old man Giygas' House and get the 'candy.'

CHAPTER V: At least I can do THIS!

On the way up the long and winding mountain road to Old Man Giygas'
house, it started to rain, and lightning shot down and hit a rock
nearby. "I could do that," Ness mumbled to himself.
"So, anyway, I can't help but notice you guys have been acting wierd
lately... I mean, I thought you LIKED Mounds bars. And the only way
you've been talking is monotone. No one does THAT."
"IT'S ALL IN YOUR HEAD. YOU ARE A MAD MAN," Poo said, changing his
pitch from a C to an E flat every other word.

CHAPTER VI: OLD MAN GIYGAS

Old Man Giygas' house wasn't really a house. It was more, like, a
mansion. Ness tried to tap the doorbell, but he ended up smashing it.
This made the song "Yellow Submarine" play over and over again endlessly.
Slowly, the huge wooden door opened itself up to reveal a really long
table stacked with about a billion pieces of candy. The sign next to
it said 'TAKE ONE.' "MMMMM. I WILL TAKE THE SKITTLES." Paula and Poo
monotoned. "I'll take 170 of these babies." Ness motioned to the
Starbursts. Suddenly, an old beat up figure with white hair in a
tuxedo came in through the ceiling.
"You will if you escape a life," Old Man Giygas said.
"No, you, like, mean, like, mean, like, alive." Ness stuttered.
Old Man Giygas paused. "You're right."

CHAPTER VII: FIGHT SCENE

Paula and Poo turned to Ness and raised their laser guns.
"Hey, uh, guys?" Ness twiddled. "YOU WILL BECOME ONE OF US," Paula and
Poo said. "Um.." "Come on Ness, don't you want to be cool?"
Old Man Giygas rasped. "If you want to be cool, you have to smoke, like
us." Paula, Poo, and Old Man Giygas motioned toward a cigarette.
"No way!" Ness said. "Smoking isn't cool, it's bad for you! It can cause
cancer and lung damage and all that jazz!" "WOW, I DIDN'T KNOW THAT."
Paula said. Old Man Giygas opened his mouth, and the paused and thought.
"Why, you are absolutely right! I guess I have a reason to quit now!"
Old Man Giygas picked up all the cigarettes and threw them away.
After he did that, Poo and Paula turned back to normal, and Old Man Giygas
turned into a beautiful princess. "Wow Ness, you taught us a valuable lesson!"
Poo multitoned. "When you guys were smoking, you were really lame. If you
stayed lame like that, that would be really... SCARY!"
Everybody laughed and ate all the candy and got blood poisoning from too much
sugar and died.

EPILOGUE:

Their bodies were never found. But they say, every Halloween, at Old Man Giygas'
house, if you go in, candy will force itself down your throat and will CHOKE!
Also, Jeff is now one of the 13 wonders of the world.

THE END
